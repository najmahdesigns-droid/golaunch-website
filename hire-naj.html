How I work
01I understand the bigger picture. Before building, I look at the offer, customer journey, team, platforms, and business objective, so the system supports the broader business instead of solving one isolated problem.
02I take ownership. I don't need every project divided into small instructions. Give me the goal, resources, and constraints, I can map the work, identify missing pieces, and move it forward.
03I bring ideas and solutions. When I notice a bottleneck, missed opportunity, or weak customer experience, I raise it with a practical recommendation, focused on revenue, efficiency, delivery, or customer experience.
04I combine strategy and implementation. I can discuss priorities with leadership, turn decisions into an execution plan, and build the pages, workflows, integrations, and documentation myself.
05I use performance data. After implementation, I review results and identify what to adjust. Funnels, launches, and workflows should keep improving based on how customers actually interact with them.
06I build for consistency. Clear naming conventions, reusable templates, documented workflows, and quality-control processes, so systems are easier to manage, delegate, and expand.
07I test my work. Pages, forms, payments, triggers, tags, email links, personalization fields, integrations, and mobile responsiveness, because "published" and "working properly" are not always the same thing.
